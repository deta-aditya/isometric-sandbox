Controls

V select: click an item to pick it up, click again to drop it
Drag a box to mark everything in it; Shift + click adds or removes, Shift + box adds
With things marked, click anywhere to move them all; R turns them in place; Esc or right click lets go
The toggles in the tool box (or Shift+1-4) choose which kinds select picks
Ctrl+C / X copy or cut what is marked; Ctrl+V paste, click to place copies, Esc to stop
Blueprints: mark things, then Save as blueprint in the Blueprints tray; click a card (or its number) to place copies
In select, Q builds more of the item under the cursor
While moving: R turn (Shift+R back), Esc put back, Del delete
I picker: click anything placed to build more of it
Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y redo
Click place, drag paint tiles or build walls along grid lines
Walls: M switches between line mode and square mode
Paints: click or drag along walls to paint the side you point at (glass gets a tint); Plain strips it
Shift + click fills: a floor spreads over the same floor (walls stop it), a paint along the wall over the same paint
Shift + Ctrl + click fills over every floor or paint
Openings: click a wall to add a door, window or arch
B bulldoze: click, or drag a box, to remove; Shift + click clears a whole room
While bulldozing, 1-4 (or the toggles) choose what goes: scenery, furniture, flooring, building
Right drag, Space + drag, WASD or arrows: pan, wheel: zoom
Q E category, Tab next tab, 1-9 item, R turn a quarter (Shift+R back)
Esc or right click while building: back to select
[ ] change floor, Esc cancel or close tray
G grid lines on or off (they stay faint while building)
, . turn the view to see the other sides (or the turn buttons)
